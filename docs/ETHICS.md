# Ethics and Privacy

## Responsible Use

This project is for academic exploratory analysis. It should be used to understand broad job-market patterns, not to rank individuals, infer protected attributes, or make hiring decisions.

## Data Sensitivity

Job postings can include company names, application links, descriptions, salary ranges, and work-arrangement details. Even when data is public or assignment-provided, it should be handled carefully.

## Guardrails

- Do not commit raw datasets.
- Do not publish application URLs or source files unless sharing is approved.
- Avoid claims that overstate causation.
- Explain assumptions behind salary normalisation and outlier filtering.
- Consider platform bias when interpreting job-market trends.
- Attribute the academic context and original work clearly.

## Interpretation Risks

- High views do not guarantee high applicant interest.
- Low application counts may reflect missing data, role specificity, or external application flows.
- Remote-work availability may vary by industry, seniority, and employer policy.
- Salary fields may be missing, inconsistent, or expressed in different pay periods.
