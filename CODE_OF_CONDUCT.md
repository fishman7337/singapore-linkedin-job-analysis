# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We commit to making this project a respectful, inclusive, and constructive learning space for students, educators, and contributors.

## Expected Behaviour

- Use welcoming and professional language.
- Give feedback that is specific, evidence-based, and kind.
- Respect academic integrity and attribution.
- Be mindful when discussing personal, institutional, or dataset-sensitive information.
- Assume good intent while still addressing mistakes clearly.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Sharing private credentials, student records, or restricted datasets.
- Plagiarism, unattributed copying, or misrepresentation of analysis work.
- Repeatedly disrupting project discussions or reviews.

## Reporting

If you notice conduct issues, raise them with the repository maintainer or the appropriate academic supervisor. Reports should include what happened, where it happened, and any relevant links or screenshots.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behaviour conflicts with this code. Academic misconduct concerns should follow the institution's official process.
